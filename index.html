How it's made

01

Collect

Post-consumer PET bottles are gathered and sorted by recycling partners.

02

Flake

Bottles are washed, labels removed, and shredded into clean flakes.

03

Spin

Flakes melt into pellets, then spin into strong polyester yarn.

04

Weave

Yarn is woven into 600D ripstop, then coated to shed rain.

05

Build

Panels are cut, bar-tacked at stress points, and fitted with sealed zips.
